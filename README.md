# FendousBio.ai

A focused landing page for two responsible health and biotechnology AI products:

- **Healthcare Analytics** — mobile early-stage health monitoring, including blood-group detection and glucose-level prediction.
- **BioPathfinder** — AI-assisted drug discovery and biological research support.

## Netlify deployment

This repository is configured for Netlify through `netlify.toml`. Connect the repository and deploy from the `main` branch. No build command is required.

## Important

The products are in development and are not medical devices. They do not provide medical advice or replace qualified clinical or scientific judgement.
